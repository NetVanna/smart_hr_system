# SmartHR - Complete SaaS HR Platform Roadmap

SmartHR is a modern **HR SaaS platform designed for Cambodian businesses**. It provides employee management, attendance tracking, payroll, and HR analytics with specific localization for the Cambodian market.

---

# 👑 1. Super Admin (Platform Owner)
The **Super Admin** manages the entire SaaS ecosystem.

## 🌐 Platform Management
- **Company Management**: Create, Edit, Suspend companies.
- **Limit Controls**: Employee limits, storage limits per company.
- **Geofencing**: Configure authorized office locations and radii globally.
- **Global Config**: System-wide settings and maintenance modes.

## 👥 User Management
- **Universal Visibility**: View all users across the platform.
- **Security**: Reset company admin passwords, suspend accounts, and view audit logs.
- **Roles**: Manage system-wide role permissions.

## 💰 SaaS Billing System
- **Plans**: Starter ($15/mo), Business ($39/mo), Enterprise ($99/mo).
- **Billing Flow**: Free trial management, KHQR receipt verification, and invoice history.
- **Reminders**: Automated subscription renewal notifications.

## 📊 Analytics Dashboard
- MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) tracking.
- Company registration trends and trial-to-paid conversion rates.
- Total platform user growth.

## 🎫 Support & Marketing
- **Ticket System**: Direct help desk for Technical, Billing, and Feature requests.
- **Campaigns**: Announcements, marketing emails, and promotion alerts.

---

# 🏢 2. Company Admin (HR / Manager)
Company admins manage their organization's daily operations.

## 👥 Employee Management
- **Profiles**: Photos, join dates, contract types, salary history, and personal info.
- **Org Structure**: Manage Departments (HR, IT, Sales) and Positions (Manager, Staff, etc.).

## 🕒 Attendance & Leave
- **Multi-Factor Attendance**: QR Code, GPS Geofencing, and Face Recognition.
- **Time Tracking**: Late detection, Overtime (OT), and Shift rotation logic.
- **Leave Management**: Annual, Sick, Maternity, and Unpaid leave workflows with balance tracking.

## 💰 Payroll & Expenses
- **Dual-Currency Payroll**: Automated USD/KHR salary calculation with PDF payslips.
- **Allowances & Deductions**: Transport, Meal, Tax, and Late penalties.
- **Expense Tracking**: Employee submission → Manager approval → Reimbursement.

## ⭐ Performance & Growth
- **Evaluations**: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual reviews based on quality and productivity.
- **Asset Tracking**: Monitor company equipment (Laptops, Phones) assigned to staff.
- **Training**: Track employee certifications and mandatory training programs.

## 📊 HR Analytics
- **Reports**: Export Attendance, Late records, Payroll costs, and Budget reports to Excel/PDF.

---

## 👨‍💼 3. Staff (Employees)
Employees interact primarily through the **Mobile App**.

- **Smart Attendance**: QR check-in with GPS and Face verification.
- **Self-Service**: 
    - Submit leave requests with photo attachments.
    - View and download monthly Digital Payslips.
    - Update personal contact and emergency information.
- **Company Feed**: View news, announcements, and holiday alerts.
- **Digital ID**: QR-based employee ID for access and verification.

---

## 🌍 Cambodian Localization
- **Language**: Khmer / English UI toggle.
- **Currency**: Native USD and KHR dual-currency support.
- **Compliance**: Cambodian Public Holiday calendar integration.
- **Payments**: Native KHQR (Bakong/ABA) integration.
- **Alerts**: Real-time Telegram bot notifications for managers.

---

## 🚀 Vision
SmartHR aims to become the leading HR SaaS platform in Cambodia, combining international HR best practices with local requirements like KHQR payments and Khmer language support.
